## 2. Ranked pain points (strongest evidence first)

### P1. 図面・見積・受注がFAX/紙で、見積が社長やベテランに集中 (Drawings, quotes and orders on fax/paper; quoting bottlenecked on one person)

Evidence:
- AI inside survey reported by MONOist, 2024-09-10: 95.2% of manufacturers exchange paper documents in order handling; 73.3% receive orders by FAX; 60.4% type FAX orders in manually; 88.4% want to stop FAX; top reason they cannot (63.0%) is "取引先がFAXだから". https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2409/10/news066.html (vendor-run survey; sample size not stated).
- note, Takumi Giken 導入事例 #2, 2025-05-21, 株式会社クライン (small-lot metal parts): "月に200～300件の見積依頼が入ることもあり、1日の半分以上を見積作業に費やす日も珍しくなかった"; CEO: "月に200件以上の見積を対応する時期は、1日の半分以上を見積に費やすこともありました". https://note.com/takumigiken/n/n82fccd2c387e (read; vendor case study).
- note, Takumi Giken case study (via search): "quotation request arrived, we would first print it on paper, take it to the shop floor, and handwrite the man-hours and material sizes". https://note.com/takumigiken/n/n332595cd86bc . Vendor case study. UNVERIFIED (snippet).
- note by BLUEPRINT CTO, 2023-04-28, 150 Hatena bookmarks: drawing search took "20分、30分"; Google Drive "めちゃくちゃ使いづらい" for shop staff; existing production systems are project-chronological, shops think drawing-first; full systems cost "数千万円クラス". Target: 40,000–50,000 shops with up to 50 staff. https://note.com/pd_m/n/n983e40f03fde (read). Product = ズメーン.
- note, 2025-05-24, 町工場 専務: a shop declined to quote ("硬くて削れない"); other quotes came back near double. https://note.com/dongonzinza/n/nd06ac25bcfcf (read).

Current workaround and why tools fail: FAX + Excel + the president's memory. CADDi Drawer, ズメーン and Aperza already do drawing search; FAX OCR products exist. They store drawings, but do not capture *why* a price was set, and they assume the shop will digitise its whole archive first.

Who suffers: 10–50 person job shops, especially the one person who quotes.

App idea (avoid generic FAX OCR, which was already evaluated): **"Mitsumori Memory" — quote-reason capture for steel-plate shearing shops.** When a FAX/PDF drawing comes in, the app extracts plate thickness, material and outline (simple parsing plus manual fix). It finds the three most similar past jobs and shows the price *and the voice note the quoter left* ("SS400 t22 は熔断後に歪み取りで+15分"). Each new quote requires a 10-second reason note. TSUNAGU: past↔future (the president's pricing logic outlives him), data↔data (drawing ↔ quote ↔ ANS WIN cutting result).
- Daishin/Nagasaki fit: high. Shearing shops are ANS WIN's 2000+ customer base; an export from 切断管理/実績管理 could feed actual cut times back into quotes.
- Originality 5/10 (drawing search exists; reason-capture angle is newer). Feasibility 7/10.
- Honest demo data: 30 self-drawn simple plate parts (flanges, gussets) in DXF/PDF, prices computed from a stated public formula (material ¥/kg × weight + cut length × rate), and the team's own voice notes. Label as synthetic.

### P2. 技能継承・属人化 ("If that person leaves, the factory stops")

Evidence:
- Yahoo!知恵袋 cluster on "見て覚えろ" and no manuals (10+ threads in one search). Examples: "工場なのに作業標準書が無いのって普通ですか？" 2022-04-05, 5 answers, one calls it "異常レベル", another says small shops keep it in the owner's head. https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13259862253 (read). Also https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q11130309531 ("工場って仕事を教えない人が多すぎませんか"), https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q10294541595 , https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14220072029 , https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14230896254 (maintenance staff not learning after a year). Dates of the others not checked.
- note, 2026-02-28, Solvact: 55-year-old veteran is the only one who can write programs for new parts and quote from drawings; lots of 1–5 pieces; paper manuals "作られたその瞬間から陳腐化"; OJT = "教育の放棄". https://note.com/solvact_info/n/n5c7b812c2c03 (read; author sells AI CAM).
- note, 2024-08-29, "84歳社長が死んだら終わる会社": "社長が死んだらどうなるか、誰にも分からない"; veterans retire in about five years, no one to inherit. https://note.com/real_field_game/n/n700c01c1799b (read).
- note, 2025-05-24: "技術をもった職人たちが技術を伝承せぬまま、引退している". https://note.com/dongonzinza/n/nd06ac25bcfcf (read).
- note (construction): 野原グループ 2025 survey, over 90% of firms "技術継承に不安". https://note.com/mino11293/n/nf2876c6ab50c . UNVERIFIED (snippet; survey not opened).
- Hatena text search "技能継承": 30 entries, top 42 users (AI agents for tacit knowledge, 2026-05-15). Media-driven, not forum-driven. https://b.hatena.ne.jp/search/text?q=技能継承
- MONOist 2024-06-27: 技能継承 is "一回やれば終わり" not, needs data loop. https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2406/27/news083.html

Why tools fail: video-manual tools (tebiki, etc.) need someone to film and edit. Paper SOPs go stale every job. Veterans have no time.

App idea (not a skill-gap map; anchor to things): **"Machine Diary / 機械の遺言" — QR on each machine and drawing.** A veteran taps the QR and speaks 20 seconds while working ("このプラズマはノズル交換後は速度を5%落とす"). The app transcribes it, tags it to the machine and material, translates it, and surfaces it to the next operator who scans the same QR before the same job type. Weekly "unanswered questions" from juniors go to the veteran's phone as yes/no prompts. TSUNAGU: people↔things, past↔future.
- Fit: Daishin sells to shops whose cutting conditions are exactly this tacit knowledge. Nagasaki: shipyard subcontractors losing welders.
- Originality 6/10. Feasibility 8/10.
- Demo data: 5 printed QR codes on lab objects standing in for machines; 15 notes recorded by the team from public cutting-condition guides (cite source); translation output shown live.

### P3. 外国人材への指示・安全教育が伝わらない (Instructions and safety briefings not understood by foreign workers)

Evidence:
- 知恵袋 2022-06-11, Vietnamese intern on precision machines: "いつも謎解きゲーム状態"; manuals only in Japanese; 4,168 views, 9 answers; top answer (13 なるほど) says use やさしい日本語 and translated manuals. https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13263117113 (read; older than preferred window).
- 知恵袋, further threads (dates not all checked, roughly 2019–2025; titles read from search results): "日本語が通じない外国人実習生について" https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q12330697487 ; "外国人の新人の教育を担当しています" https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13327484148 ; "建設会社の下請会社なのですが、外国人技能実習生を雇っています" https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q10238693490 . Answer content attributed via search summary, thread not opened, so not pinned to a specific thread: advice "短く・具体的に・一つずつ"; workers say わかりました without understanding; an intern sent alone to a site did off-plan work, was injured (労災) and the 機構 issued an improvement order; Myanmar intern with N2 still not following: https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q10324457005 ; whole factory "気疲れ": https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q13293300058 . At least 10 threads in one search.
- Injury rates 令和6年 (2024): 技能実習 3.98 and 特定技能 3.91 per 1,000 vs all workers 2.3. MHLW Shizuoka Labour Bureau PDF https://jsite.mhlw.go.jp/shizuoka-roudoukyoku/content/contents/002261080.pdf and Business & Human Rights summary https://www.business-humanrights.org/ja/最新ニュース/日本外国人労働者の労災が13年連続で前年を上回り技能実習特定技能の死傷者の割合が高いと報告される . Seen in search snippets; MHLW corrected some 令和6 figures on 2025-08-22, so check the final table before citing. PARTLY VERIFIED.
- note posts (2024–2026, mostly support vendors): text-only safety manuals "文字が読めても意味が分からない"; translation apps "誤訳が多く事故につながる"; pre-arrival videos watched "大丈夫" but terms and steps still confused. https://note.com/rso_soeasy/n/n45d46e680bdd , https://note.com/smilinkxus/n/n28a41039ff23 , https://note.com/just_badger7680/n/n908eb01b6782 . Vendor-ish.
- Shipbuilding: 特定技能1号 in 造船・舶用 9,957 people at 2025-03 (preliminary); welding about 77% of 1号; shortfall still about 36,900 after measures. MLIT via search: https://www.mlit.go.jp/maritime/content/002026775.pdf . UNVERIFIED (snippet).
- Nagasaki Prefecture shipbuilding training doc: https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/shared/uploads/2025/06/1749610158.pdf (snippet only).
- MHLW multilingual safety manuals exist (official, free): https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_56354.html

Why tools fail: translation apps translate words, not site jargon ("玉掛け", "ケレン"). Video tools do not check understanding. Briefings are one-way.

App idea: **"Wakatta? — comprehension-checked safety briefing."** The foreman speaks today's instruction in Japanese. The app rewrites it into やさしい日本語, translates it to the worker's language (Bengali, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Nepali) using a shop jargon glossary, and adds a pictogram. Each worker answers one tap quiz on their own phone ("Where do you stand when the crane lifts?"). The foreman sees who has not understood before work starts. Unclear words feed the glossary. TSUNAGU: people↔people across language.
- Fit: very high for Nagasaki (shipyard welding subcontractors, 水産加工) and for the prefecture's Bangladesh programme. Bangladeshi students can credibly build the Bengali side and test it themselves. Daishin fit: medium (their 安否安全管理 line).
- Originality 6/10 (translation exists; per-person comprehension check plus jargon glossary is the novel part). Feasibility 9/10.
- Demo data: 10 real instructions taken from the MHLW multilingual manuals (cite page), glossary of 30 terms, team members as test workers. Do not show fake injury statistics.

### P4. 設備保全の記録が紙・Excelで、故障対応が人頼み (Maintenance records on paper/Excel; breakdown response depends on one person)

Evidence:
- MONOist series "設備保全DXの現状と課題" part 3, 2025-06-24: Excel persists from habit, cost and lack of IT staff; roughly two in three large firms still manage maintenance mainly in Excel (garbled text); equipment counts rise while headcount is flat. https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2506/24/news007.html ; part 1, 2025-02-17: https://monoist.itmedia.co.jp/mn/articles/2502/17/news002.html . Written with a maintenance-system vendor (Yachiyo / MENTENA). Vendor-adjacent.
- 知恵袋: new maintenance staff fear "自分の時に対応できないトラブルが起きたら"; advice is to read circuit and ladder diagrams and call the maker. https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q12291983357 , https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q14327612064 , https://detail.chiebukuro.yahoo.co.jp/qa/question_detail/q11298736051 (old machines, repairs they cannot do). Dates not checked.
- Hatena "設備 故障 属人化": 9 entries, none from small factories. Weak forum signal.

Why tools fail: CMMS products (MENTENA, Fracta, etc.) need asset registers set up first; small shops never finish setup.

App idea: **"Kosho Karte Lite" — breakdown log in 3 taps plus similar-failure search.** Scan machine QR, take photo, speak symptom; app suggests past fixes for that machine and maker; MTBF per machine falls out automatically. TSUNAGU: data↔data, past↔future.
- Fit: Daishin already sells 保全カルテ and 遠隔現場支援 for power plants. A lightweight mobile front end that could export to such a system shows awareness. Risk: looks like their product. Pitch as "field capture app for small shops".
- Originality 4/10. Feasibility 8/10. Consider merging with P2 (same QR, two note types).
- Demo data: 3 lab devices (printer, 3D printer, fan) as machines, 20 real breakdowns from the team's own lab history.

TDB counts 67,949 and says the profitable share fell below 50% for the first time. https://www.tsr-net.co.jp/data/detail/1202284_1527.html , https://www.zaikei.co.jp/releases/3254901/ (snippets; not opened). UNVERIFIED in detail.
- 中小企業庁 supply-chain flyer: replacing a supplier or bringing work in-house "時間を要します"; act early. https://www.chusho.meti.go.jp/zaimu/shoukei/download/supply-chain_chirashi.pdf ; Yamanashi case of a business transfer that kept parts flowing: https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/documents/80119/1.pdf (snippets).
- note 2025-05-24 (専務): closures keep happening, "跡継ぎがいない"; a veteran wanted to retire "with his machine"; hard-material work now hard to place. https://note.com/dongonzinza/n/nd06ac25bcfcf (read).
- note 2024-08-29 (84歳社長): see P2.

Why tools fail: M&A platforms (BATONZ, TRANBI) sell whole companies. Most tiny shops are not sellable; their value is a machine, a skill and five customers.

App idea: **"Tsugite (継ぎ手) — capability handover map."** A retiring shop lists its machines, special skills (e.g. hard-material cutting), active customers and drawings. Nearby shops and young owners see which capabilities are about to vanish in their area and can claim a machine, a customer or an apprenticeship slot. Shown on a Nagasaki/Sasebo map. TSUNAGU: communities, past↔future.
- Fit: Nagasaki's shipbuilding subcontractor base is ageing; prefecture cares about keeping it. Daishin: medium.
- Originality 7/10. Feasibility 7/10.
- Demo data: fictional shops clearly labelled as fictional, placed on real industrial-area map tiles; machine types from public catalogues.

They ran a PoC with a simple Excel tool on selected irregular remnants, with a roadmap to an external sales platform. https://www.kyushu.meti.go.jp/seisaku/jyoho/jirei/dx_quest/2025/260302_1_6.pdf (403 on fetch; content from search summary only). UNVERIFIED.
- Kanagawa Prefecture slide deck (2022-03): remnants "加工業の特性上、必ず発生する"; left unmanaged and dumped in bulk at year-end. https://www.pref.kanagawa.jp/documents/75083/1kamisiro.pdf (PDF password-protected; snippet only). UNVERIFIED.
- Osaka Prefecture shearing-industry report: shearing is key in steel distribution; gas cutting needs skilled operators who are declining. https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/documents/30624/syaringu.pdf (snippet).
- No forum voices found (note searches returned only hobby/upcycling posts).

Why tools fail: nesting software (including CAD/CAM like ANS WIN) can use remnants only if the remnant is registered with exact size and location. Registration is the step nobody does.

App idea: **"Zanzai Snap" — photo-measure remnant registry.** Worker photographs a remnant on the floor next to a reference marker; the app estimates outline and size (OpenCV), records thickness and grade by tap, prints a QR label, and gives a rack location. Exports a remnant list that a nesting/CAM tool could import. Optional: list surplus remnants to nearby shops. TSUNAGU: people↔things, data↔data (floor ↔ CAM).
- Fit: highest Daishin fit of all items (direct complement to ANS WIN / 熔断くんWIN for 2000+ shearing customers). Sustainability angle for the prefecture.
- Originality 7/10. Feasibility 6/10 (photo measurement is the risk; fall back to manual entry with photo).
- Demo data: cardboard or plywood offcuts with an A4 marker, measured live; show error vs tape measure honestly.
