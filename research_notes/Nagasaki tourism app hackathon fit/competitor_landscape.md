# Competitor landscape: what a Nagasaki tourism app would compete with (as of Nov 2026)

## Q1. What do global/national players already offer a Nagasaki visitor (Google Maps, "Discover Japan", NAVITIME, Tripadvisor, Klook/KKday, AI planners)?

### Takeaway
The general "find a shop/hotel/restaurant, ask an AI, plan a day" job is now strongly served. Google Maps' Gemini "Ask Maps" (マップに相談) launched in Japan in Aug 2026 and added itinerary building in Nov 2026. NAVITIME has had a generative-AI itinerary planner since 2024. Tripadvisor is reachable inside AI assistants. An "AI chatbot to find shops/hotels" is therefore the weakest differentiator in the proposed bundle.

### Cited Findings
**Google Maps**
- Google launched Gemini-powered "Ask Maps" in March 2026 (US and India first). It answers open-ended questions and personalizes results from past searches and saved places. — [MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/03/12/google-maps-gemini-integration/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/12/google-brings-more-gemini-ai-to-navigation-with-ask-maps-feature.html); [TechCrunch](https://www.techcrunch.com/2026/03/12/google-maps-is-getting-an-ai-ask-maps-feature-and-upgraded-immersive-navigation/)
- The Japanese version, 「マップに相談」, launched in Japan on 2026-08-07 with a staged rollout over several weeks. It handles queries such as "a place where I can buy coffee and charge my phone". It was later linked with Gmail through "personal intelligence". — [Google Japan blog](https://blog.google/intl/ja-jp/products/connect-communicate/japanese-ask-maps/); [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2131621.html); [PC Watch](https://pc.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/2131967.html)
- The mid-Nov 2026 holiday update added four Ask Maps features:
  - step-by-step itinerary creation
  - "Know before you go" route guidance
  - reservation search with phone assistance
  - real-time availability search within saved lists
  Two of them (itinerary creation and the saved-list availability search) work in every market where Ask Maps is offered. — [Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/maps/ask-maps-holiday-travel-features-2026/); a third-party summary says some features are still US-only: [jetstream.blog](https://jetstream.blog/en/google-maps-ask-maps-holiday-travel-features-2026/)
- Ask Maps can split a trip into daily plans and pin stops on the map. Users can share an itinerary or export it to Google Docs. — [GPTunneL summary](https://www.gptunnel.ru/en/blog/google-ask-maps) (aggregator; lower confidence)
- No 2026 source was retrieved on Local Guides, reviews or lists specifically. These are long-standing core features (see Gaps).

**"Discover Japan": three candidate products**
- *Discover Japan magazine app* (seller: DENTSU MAGAZINE). A free digital magazine with in-app issue purchases covering crafts, food, festivals and scenery. The sample issues on its listing date from 2015–2018, so the app looks stale. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id972428725)
- *JNTO "Japan Official Travel App"*, launched 2017-09-21 in English, Korean and Chinese.
  - NAVITIME provided route search.
  - LIVE JAPAN, All About and Time Out Tokyo supplied content.
  - Gurunavi supplied restaurants.
  This is likely what a user means by an "official Japan travel app". — [Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/english/jnto-releases-an-official-app-for-international-visitors-working-together-with-a-variety-of-private-sectors); [honichi](https://honichi.com/news/2017/09/22/jntojapanofficialtravelapp/); [Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/blog/the-jnto-has-released-a-new-travel-app-to-guide-you-around-the-country-092517). JNTO's useful-apps page: [japan.travel](https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/useful-apps/)
- *Discover Another Japan* is a separate, unrelated app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1530307213)

**Japan Travel by NAVITIME**
- Features: Explore (guides and articles), Route Search, Map and offline spot search, Plan. Free with in-app purchases. Now sells eSIMs. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id686373726)
- A generative-AI trip planner was added in Sep 2024. It builds 3–10-day itineraries through chat. — [Travel Voice EN](https://www.travelvoice.jp/english/navitime-japan-a-leading-navigation-app-provider-adds-an-ai-based-travel-planning-function-on-the-navigation-website-for-inbound-travelers)
- Since Feb 2025, users can photograph a station or bus-stop sign so the app checks they are boarding the right vehicle.
  - Premium costs $14.49 for 30 days, $29.99 for 90 days or $109.99 for a year.
  - The site also lists an "AI Assistant (Early Access)".
  — [NAVITIME press release](https://corporate.navitime.co.jp/en/topics/topics/202502/18_5883.html)
- Available in 5 languages. A Sep 2024 review said the app did not cover all of Japan. It listed Tokyo, Osaka, Aichi, Fukuoka and Hokkaido, so Nagasaki coverage was unclear then. This is older information. — [Unseen Japan](https://unseen-japan.com/navitime-travel-app-japan/)
- NAVITIME's domestic Travel app also lets users paste itineraries from ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude to import spots. This was reported Sep 2026. — [Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20260916-160522)

**Tripadvisor / AI planners / Klook / KKday**
- As of Apr 2026, Tripadvisor and Viator are available inside Claude for comparing options, then clicking out to book. — [Tripadvisor mediaroom](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/2026-04-23-How-AI-Is-Changing-the-Way-We-Plan-Travel)
- Tripadvisor added an AI product builder for experience operators in Jun 2026. — [Tripadvisor mediaroom](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/2026-06-10-Building-Whats-Next-in-Experiences-Summer-Update)
- Tripadvisor has an AI itinerary generator. — [Marketech APAC](https://marketech-apac.com/tripadvisor-launches-ai-powered-travel-planning-generator/)
- Trip.com has an AI planning hub that returns a bookable trip from 3 questions. — [Skift](https://skift.com/2025/08/28/trip-com-groups-ai-shrinks-planning-work-answer-three-questions-get-a-bookable-trip/); [Globetrender](https://globetrender.com/2025/11/16/trip-com-unveils-ai-travel-planner-tool/)

### Inferences
- A general AI chatbot for local shops and hotels duplicates Google's 「マップに相談」, which now runs in Japanese inside the default map app. Judges from Japanese tech firms will likely know this.
- An AI angle helps only if it uses data Google lacks: curated meibutsu and souvenir knowledge, peace-history narratives, stamp-rally state, or local-community posts.

### Gaps
- No Klook or KKday Nagasaki-specific data was retrieved. Both are known to sell Japan activity and transport tickets, but this was not verified here.
- No current (2026) source on the status of the JNTO Japan Official Travel App.
- Google Local Guides, reviews and lists: no 2026 source was fetched (core features since well before 2020).

## Q2. What official/local Nagasaki tourism apps and sites exist?

### Takeaway
Nagasaki already has many fragmented official digital tools. Each is single-purpose and mostly Japanese-first:
- MaaS and ticketing (STLOCAL)
- hotel concierge (VERY)
- an island guide (Goto)
- a theme-park app (Huis Ten Bosch)
- school-trip peace AR (長崎平和AR, MuuMu AR, student-made NAGASAKI PEACE DOOR)
- a 2010 map-based A-bomb archive (Nagasaki Archive)

None combines peace heritage, meibutsu community and gamified travel. None found offers an interactive 3D model of Peace Park or the Peace Statue.

### Cited Findings
**General / MaaS**
- **STLOCAL (Zenrin)**: launched 2022-03-16 as Nagasaki City's first tourism MaaS app.
  - Plan trips and buy e-tickets for transport, facilities and activities.
  - Find spots and deals by area.
  - Save spots to build a route.
  — [Zenrin](https://www.zenrin.co.jp/information/product/220316.html); [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000053.000067172.html); [Goto tabinet](https://goto.nagasaki-tabinet.com/feature/application)
  - It still runs in 2026. It hosts Nagasaki City's "GO! GO! 長崎スタンプラリー" (2026-08-07 to 2027-03-31) at about 250 spots, including restaurants, souvenir shops, hotels and Nagasaki Stadium City tenants. Checking in at 3 or more spots enters a lottery for local products. — [Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20260812-160290)
  - It also sells the "ながさき観光選べるセットチケット" bundle. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000128.000067172.html)
- **VERY travel Nagasaki (talkappi)**: delivers content from the city's official site "travel nagasaki" to hotel guests. Started 2024-02-08. — per search summary; primary URL not fetched (see Gaps)
- **長崎トラベルガイドパス**: an audio-guide app with digital coupons. Release date not confirmed. — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000750.000004858.html)

**Islands and theme park**
- **まるっと！五島ガイド**: Goto City's official tourism app (iOS and Android), recently renewed.
  - Japan Heritage pages and geosites.
  - Pre-trip (旅マエ) and on-trip (旅ナカ) modes.
  - The Goto tourism site also recommends STLOCAL and 自転車NAVITIME (for Goto cycling courses).
  — [Goto tabinet](https://goto.nagasaki-tabinet.com/feature/application)
- **ハウステンボス 公式アプリ** (App Store id6450489514):
  - wait times and show schedules on a map
  - map with current location and filters
  - shortest-route guidance
  - recommended courses
  It requires GPS. — [App Store JP](https://apps.apple.com/JP/app/id6450489514). A non-official source says service began 2023-08-01 on iOS and Android. — [mwm.ai](https://mwm.ai/apps/hausutenbosu-gong-shi-apuri/6450489514)

**Peace and A-bomb heritage (closest to the "3D heiwa" idea)**
- **長崎平和ARアプリ**: made by the Nagasaki City DMO for school-trip students. It covers pre-visit, on-site and post-visit study, with AR guidance around A-bomb ruins and related sites. — [at-nagasaki DMO](https://www.at-nagasaki.jp/dmo/project/educational_tools)
- **被爆建造物等AR (MuuMu, commissioned by Nagasaki City, Apr 2023)**: works by scanning QR codes on site signboards.
  - At the hypocenter: 360° panoramas comparing today with just after the bombing.
  - At the Sanno Shrine one-legged torii: a destruction animation.
  — [PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000003.000044329.html)
- **NAGASAKI PEACE DOOR**: built by 5 Nagasaki Higashi High School students. It uses AR and 3D to let remote users feel present at sites such as Urakami Cathedral. Aimed at "people who hesitate to come". Reported about 7 months before Nov 2026. — [NBC長崎 via Yahoo](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/3539468c1e86008f7f84f34282d1c6a39e96b0c0); [docomo](https://topics.smt.docomo.ne.jp/article/nbc/region/nbc-2618476)
- **Nagasaki Archive (ナガサキ・アーカイブ)** by Hidenori Watanabe: released 2010-08-02. It maps survivor testimony and historic photos on Google Earth. — [NDL Current Awareness](https://current.ndl.go.jp/car/180209); [gendai.media](https://gendai.media/articles/-/37517); [NTT Data](https://www.nttdata.com/jp/ja/trends/data-insight/2018/071301)
- Hiroshima comparator: 「ヒロシマの記憶AR」 recreates Peace Memorial Park in AR and can be used anywhere. A Nagasaki Peace Park equivalent was not found. — [Timelooper](https://www.timelooper.co.jp/works/hiroshima-peace-ar); [hiroshimano-kioku.com](http://hiroshimano-kioku.com/)

**AI / chatbot in Nagasaki**
- 2019: Goto City trialled a chatbot, "Hanna", that guided foreign tourists to restaurants, hotels and spots. — [honichi](https://honichi.com/news/2019/04/29/nagasaki-ken-gos)
- 2023: Nagasaki Bus added an FAQ chatbot (HUE). This is not a tourism guide. — [IT Leaders](https://it.impress.co.jp/articles/-/25350)
- The prefecture runs a free multilingual phone-interpreting call centre for registered facilities. — [Nagasaki Pref PDF](https://www.pref.nagasaki.jp/shared/uploads/2025/07/1752145966.pdf)
- A research note cites a shortage of tourist information centres near famous sights and weak Korean-language support. — [NIU repository](https://niu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2000289/files/KGR19-059.pdf)

### Inferences
- The 3D or AR peace piece has partial incumbents: AR for school trips and QR panoramas. Free-to-explore 3D of Peace Park and the Peace Statue for remote or foreign users looks open, and the student project shows the idea resonates locally.
- Hackathon caution: content about A-bomb sites must be handled respectfully. Gamifying them (stamps or points at the hypocenter) could offend judges from Nagasaki Prefecture. Keep peace content educational, not point-scoring.

### Gaps
- Official primary page for the "travel nagasaki" site and VERY not fetched.
- Download and usage numbers were not found for any local app.
- Whether 長崎平和ARアプリ is on public app stores, and in which languages, was not found.
- Current live status of Nagasaki Archive was not confirmed.

## Q3. Digital stamp-rally platforms in Japan, and Nagasaki rallies specifically

### Takeaway
The Poke-lid stamp idea is already being run officially, twice over:
- The prefecture's 「ながさき照らす旅3」 (Denryu/Ampharos Poke-lids plus about 40 spots, GPS, quizzes and AI photo checks, prizes) runs 2026-08-27 to 2027-02-28.
- Pokémon GO's own Poké Lid Stamp Rally covers Kyushu, plus a separate Nagasaki GO Stamp Rally.

Nagasaki City's STLOCAL rally adds about 250 more spots. A hackathon app should not position "Poke-lid stamps" as new. It could aggregate rallies, or add a social or meibutsu layer on top.

### Cited Findings
- **ながさき照らす旅** history:
  - First rally: 2024-11-01 to 2025-02-28, using QR codes or GPS. — [Mynavi](https://news.mynavi.jp/article/20241021-3049052/); [PR TIMES launch](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000001.000150328.html); [Glover Garden](https://glover-garden.jp/event-and-news/22786/)
  - Second rally: started 2025-07-24. — [tabinet](https://www.nagasaki-tabinet.com/news/2473)
  - Third rally: 2026-08-27 to 2027-02-28. — [Kyodo PR](https://www.kyodo.co.jp/pr/2026-08-27_4030926/)
- 「ながさき照らす旅3」 details (official page):
  - 15 Denryu Poke-lids in the prefecture; 5 were added in summer 2026 at 諫早駅東口, ふれあい処つしま, 鐙瀬, 大島大橋公園 and 波佐見やきもの公園.
  - 43 stamp spots: 17 in 県南, 16 in 県北, 10 on the islands.
  - Entry is by smartphone. GPS unlocks missions, which are quizzes or a photo of the Poke-lid judged by AI.
  - Entry is through platinumaps.jp. Organiser: 長崎県観光連盟.
  - Prizes range from a Denryu manju set to ¥10,000 of Nagasaki wagyu, up to a GRAX OCEAN VILLAGE stay worth about ¥50,000.
  - Limited keychains ran out at some locations on day 1.
  - The rally page is Japanese-only.
  — [nagasaki-tabinet](https://www.nagasaki-tabinet.com/feature/pokefutarally)
- **Pokémon GO**:
  - Every Poké Lid has been a PokéStop since the first lid (2018-12-20). Gifts from these stops carry a lid-design postcard. — [Bulbapedia](https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Poké_Lid)
  - The in-game "Poké Lid Stamp Rally" started 2025-11-07 in Kyushu and Okinawa, tied to the Wild Area: Nagasaki event, with no end date. — [pokemongo.com](https://pokemongo.com/en/news/pokelidsr_kyushu_okinawa)
  - The separate Nagasaki GO Stamp Rally (from 2025-11-10) covers Nagasaki Station, Chinatown and more. Completing it gives an Ampharos encounter with a Nagasaki background. — [pokemongo.com](https://pokemongo.com/en/news/stamp-rally-nagasaki)
- **Platforms**:
  - Platinumaps (Boldlight), launched 2019, is used by more than 400 municipalities and tourism or transport operators. It includes the プラチナラリー stamp function and runs the Nagasaki Poke-lid rally. — [ASCII](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/378/4378171/)
  - Others: めぐラリー, みんラリ (141 rallies as of Mar 2025), Raund (Paak). — [search results incl. ascii.jp](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/294/4294338/), [newswitch](https://newswitch.jp/p/34517)
  - STLOCAL also runs check-in rallies (see Q2).

### Inferences
- Stamp collection is a well-served, commodity, white-label feature. Rallies are fragmented, though: separate URLs and apps, time-limited, Japanese-only. A unified "passport" across the prefecture rally, the city's STLOCAL rally and Pokémon GO lids, in English and other languages, could add value, although official data access is unlikely in 3 days.
- Do not claim the official Poke-lid rally or Pokémon IP. Using Pokémon art in a hackathon app is an IP risk.

### Gaps
- ラリーチ and furari: no data found.
- JR Kyushu digital rally specifics: not searched.
- Participation numbers for 照らす旅 1 and 2: not found.

## Q4. Local-specialty / souvenir discovery and locals-meet-tourists community services

### Takeaway
Souvenir discovery is covered by rankings (じゃらん お土産ランキング, Tabelog matome, many listicles) and e-commerce. Locals-to-tourists exchange is covered weakly. Japan's tourism agency (観光庁) is funding local-guide pilots, and small AI matching services (LocalEZ) exist. No dominant Nagasaki-specific "locals recommend their meibutsu to visitors" community was found.

### Cited Findings
- Jalan publishes a 長崎 souvenir ranking of 33 items using its own method. Top items include びわ茶, よりより, クルス, 桃カステラ, 九十九島せんぺい, 一口香 and 角煮まん. — [Jalan](https://www.jalan.net/omiyage/420000/)
- Castella listicles highlight 福砂屋 and 文明堂. — [Mynavi osusume](https://osusume.mynavi.jp/9668); [macaro-ni](https://macaro-ni.jp/43875); Tabelog matome: [tabelog](https://tabelog.com/matome/26305/)
- Rakuten hosts a Nagasaki official-shop product list (not a ranking). — [Rakuten](https://www.rakuten.co.jp/nagaoshi-select/contents/meika/)
- STLOCAL's 2026 rally includes souvenir shops as check-in spots. — [Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20260812-160290)
- LocalEZ, produced by NPO ZESDA, is a travel AI that brokers human connections between foreign visitors and residents. The article has inconsistent dates and figures. — [innovatopia](https://innovatopia.jp/ai/ai-news/110900/)
- 観光庁 local-guide programme: 10 regions were selected for FY2025 (R7) pilots, with results presented Feb 2026. — [MLIT local guide](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/seisaku_seido/local_guide.html); [MLIT topics](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/topics05_00043.html)
- Airbnb is promoting local-exchange tourism, for example in Osaka's Horie district. — [Airbnb news](https://news.airbnb.com/ja/newtourismosaka-umeda/)

### Inferences
- Rankings say *what* to buy but not *where nearby, from whom, and why*. A map that links meibutsu to the shops selling it, plus local voices, is less served. Google reviews partly cover it.
- A community needs critical mass. A 3-day hackathon cannot show real network effects, so seed it with content and pitch the cold-start plan, for example local schools and shopkeepers posting.

### Gaps
- Instagram, RED (小红书) and Tabelog Nagasaki usage stats: not searched or found.
- Voyagin (now part of Klook) and Airbnb Experiences listings for Nagasaki: not verified.

## Q5. Synthesis: well served vs. weak; why open this instead of Google Maps?

### Takeaway
Each feature taken alone has an incumbent. The open angle is a multilingual layer that ties things together:
- peace heritage you can explore remotely in 3D, respectfully
- Nagasaki-specific meibutsu and souvenir knowledge with local voices
- one passport over the fragmented, Japanese-only official rallies

The pitch has to answer "why not Google Maps + Pokémon GO + the tabinet rally" directly.

### Cited Findings (coverage map)
- **AI chatbot for shops and hotels: well served.** Google 「マップに相談」 has run in Japan since Aug 2026 ([Google JP blog](https://blog.google/intl/ja-jp/products/connect-communicate/japanese-ask-maps/)). There are also NAVITIME's AI planner ([Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/english/navitime-japan-a-leading-navigation-app-provider-adds-an-ai-based-travel-planning-function-on-the-navigation-website-for-inbound-travelers)) and Tripadvisor in Claude ([Tripadvisor](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/2026-04-23-How-AI-Is-Changing-the-Way-We-Plan-Travel)).
- **Maps of spots and souvenir shops: well served.** Google Maps and STLOCAL (about 250 spots, souvenir shops included) cover it. — [Travel Voice](https://www.travelvoice.jp/20260812-160290)
- **Poke-lid and spot stamp rally with gamification: well served, but fragmented.** Sources: the prefecture rally ([tabinet](https://www.nagasaki-tabinet.com/feature/pokefutarally)), Pokémon GO ([pokemongo.com](https://pokemongo.com/en/news/pokelidsr_kyushu_okinawa)) and the STLOCAL rally. The prefecture rally page is Japanese-only.
- **Peace AR or 3D: partly served.** School-trip AR ([DMO](https://www.at-nagasaki.jp/dmo/project/educational_tools)), on-site QR panoramas ([PR TIMES](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000003.000044329.html)) and a student AR/3D project ([NBC](https://news.yahoo.co.jp/articles/3539468c1e86008f7f84f34282d1c6a39e96b0c0)) exist. No public 3D Peace Park or Peace Statue explorer was found, unlike Hiroshima's AR ([Timelooper](https://www.timelooper.co.jp/works/hiroshima-peace-ar)).
- **Locals-to-tourists meibutsu community: weakly served.** Rankings exist ([Jalan](https://www.jalan.net/omiyage/420000/)). Matching services are early or pilot stage ([MLIT](https://www.mlit.go.jp/kankocho/seisaku_seido/local_guide.html), [innovatopia](https://innovatopia.jp/ai/ai-news/110900/)).

### Inferences
- **Why open it instead of Google Maps:**
  1. Curated Nagasaki-only meaning that Google does not model: the story behind each meibutsu, its peace and Christian/Dejima heritage context, and who the local maker is.
  2. A single multilingual passport for rallies that are now Japanese-only and split across several sites.
  3. A remote, before-the-trip 3D peace experience that also serves people who cannot visit, echoing the NAGASAKI PEACE DOOR thesis.
  4. Community content from locals, not anonymous reviews.
- **Fit with the TSUNAGU "connect" theme:** the strongest story is connecting locals with visitors and the past with the present (peace), not the AI chatbot.
- **Scope advice for 3 days:** pick one hero feature:
  - 3D peace explorer + meibutsu map + locals' posts, or
  - multilingual rally passport + meibutsu
  Treat AI chat as a thin helper grounded in the app's own curated data, not a Google competitor.
- **Risks:**
  - Pokémon IP: do not use the artwork.
  - Sensitivity: do not gamify A-bomb sites.
  - Duplication: prefecture judges know 照らす旅 well, so frame the app as complementing it, not copying it.

### Gaps
- No usage or download data for any competitor in Nagasaki, so popularity is inferred from official backing only.
- No survey data on foreign visitors' pain points in Nagasaki, beyond one research note on info-centre and Korean-language gaps ([NIU](https://niu.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/2000289/files/KGR19-059.pdf)).
