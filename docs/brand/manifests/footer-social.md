# Footer, dividers and social: manifest

Agent footer-social. Track P (Poster Modernist): cream ground, jet blocks, cobalt `#1351AA` only, white only for Nekko, `#C7C7C7` hairlines, 0 px corners, flat colour, no text. Nekko (expression `happy`, or standalone on footers) is inlined as a transformed `<g>`, sitting behind the Megane Bridge block so the flat bust cut is hidden. Bridge geometry follows `BridgeMark` in `frontend/src/ui/v4/components/Brand.tsx` (end piers, two stepped arches, reflection half discs below the waterline). Rasters come from `docs/brand/tools/render.cjs` on a cream ground. Sources were generated by scripts kept in the scratchpad (`work/footer-social/`), not in the repo.

## Known limits

- Footer strip Nekko is small on the 1920 desktop strip (224 px tall) by design; the mobile strip shows it larger.
- Between the two bridge arches the profile steps down, so a small white notch of Nekko's body shows between the two jet blocks. Intentional (the bridge silhouette), but it reads as a gap at small sizes.
- Reflection discs are cut by the image bottom in the social cards and footer strips (waterline sits near the bottom edge).
- Cream is baked into every image (no transparent variant); the footer is for cream pages only.
- Rasters are cream-flattened; the Icon-blue twin is built later by `recolor.py` from the SVGs.
