# The v4 "Poster Modernist" kit is the default UI

`frontend/src/ui/v4/` is a new kit (a copy of v3, restyled), registered in `shell/ui-registry.ts` and set as `DEFAULT_KIT`; v1, v2 and v3 stay reachable with `?ui=`. Its look is cream (`#E3E2DE`), jet black (`#141414`) and one cobalt (`#1351AA`), a hairline 12-column grid with a sticky label column, 0px corners, no shadows or gradients, heavy tight headlines (Aileron, self-hosted through `@fontsource/aileron`; its heaviest weight is 800, not 900), light theme only. A new kit rather than an edit of v3 follows [FRONTEND_ARCHITECTURE.md](../FRONTEND_ARCHITECTURE.md) A-04: a redesign replaces presentation only, so logic, routes and copy are untouched and the old kits remain as fallbacks. Two deliberate departures from the repo's accessibility floor, taken literally from the design brief: the `#7A7A7A` label colour on cream is about 3.3:1 and the `#C7C7C7` hairlines about 1.3:1 (below the 4.5:1 and 3:1 bars), so the A-07 pre-demo checklist does not fully pass for v4 until those two tokens are darkened (one edit in `ui/v4/tokens.css`: `--label`, `--rule`). Safety pictograms keep the JIS/ISO safety colours (red, yellow, blue, green) because the safety-signs course teaches them; all other art is cream, black and cobalt.
