# Personal accounts with name + PIN, not shared role PINs

Every Foreman, Worker and Community Poster has their own `users` row and signs in by picking their name from the shop's or district's roster (reached through the code printed on QR stickers) and typing a personal PIN: 4 digits for Workers, 6 for Foremen and Posters. The PIN is stored as argon2id of an HMAC of the PIN with a server pepper; five wrong PINs lock the account for 15 minutes. Foremen create Worker and Foreman accounts with a first PIN that must be changed at first sign-in, so the Foreman never knows a working PIN, and a Foreman's reset revokes every session of that user (lost phone). Login returns the opaque Bearer session of [ADR 0005](0005-bearer-session-tokens.md), now tied to a user: 30 days on a Worker's phone, 12 hours for Foremen and Posters. Machine Panels get a read-only device session issued by a Foreman, and Senpai keep a link token, now hashed, expiring and revocable. The Worker in every Check, scan, Ask and report is taken from the session, never from the request body. We rejected the shared role PINs of v1 because an unauthenticated `worker_id` lets anyone forge a Sign-off (the safety record the product exists for) and a shared Foreman PIN cannot say who approved a Note Version. We rejected email and password because shop-floor Workers often have neither a work email nor the Japanese to recover a password, and a name list plus a short PIN is what a low-literacy user can do with gloves on.
