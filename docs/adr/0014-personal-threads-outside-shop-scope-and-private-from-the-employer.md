# Personal threads sit outside shop scope and are private from the employer

Talk Threads (a Worker's own record of a real-life conversation with someone who is not in the app) and Contact Threads (a chat with someone connected by QR) belong to their members, not to a shop. Every other Thread, Machine and Check is shop-scoped with composite foreign keys ([ADR 0009](0009-shop-and-district-scopes.md)), but a Contact can work for another employer, so personal threads get their own user-scoped tables and are never joined to shop data. Neither the Foreman nor the employer can read them, they carry no Wakatta Status or Check Questions, and nothing from them reaches the Support Log or the Learning History; a Worker who wants a Meet-up counted logs it separately. Transcripts of what the other person said are saved like any chat message. This was chosen knowing that the MHLW information-security guide for care providers treats most care information as sensitive personal data and discourages personal devices and external cloud AI; the mitigations are a one-time notice before first use and deletion of a message or a whole thread. A QR carries an opaque, revocable token, never a user id or PIN. Scanning shows the other person's name and language, the scanner confirms, and either side can block. Rejected: shop-scoped personal threads (breaks friendships across employers and the "aside from work" goal), Foreman-visible summaries (Workers would stop using it if it felt monitored), session-only transcripts (less useful for review), and guest links for people without an account (a new unauthenticated surface).
