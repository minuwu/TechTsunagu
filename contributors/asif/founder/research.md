## 1. 特定技能1号 義務的支援 (mandatory support), records, and the periodic report

### 1.1 The 10 mandatory support items and the record each one needs

The Immigration Services Agency (ISA) lists 10 mandatory items (義務的支援１０項目). The employer (特定技能所属機関) must carry out all of them, either itself or by contracting a 登録支援機関. A 登録支援機関 must be able to carry out the whole plan; it cannot register to do only part of it, and it cannot subcontract the work.
Source: ISA, 「１号特定技能外国人支援・登録支援機関について」 (no date on page, retrieved 2026-11-22). https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/policies/ssw/supportssw.html

The "Record to keep" column comes from 特定技能外国人受入れに関する運用要領, 令和8年10月 edition (updated 2026-10-19), 第5章, 「支援の実施状況に係る文書の作成等」, pp.86–88. Every record must also hold the worker's name, date of birth, nationality/region, sex and residence card number.
https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001468647.pdf

| # | Item (ISA wording) | What it is | Record to keep (運用要領 pp.86–88) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 事前ガイダンス | A briefing after the contract is signed and before the visa application, in person or by video call. It covers working conditions, the activity, entry procedures, and whether any deposit is collected. | Staff member who ran it (including the interpreter), date/time and place, content, and method. Keep the **事前ガイダンスの確認書 (参考様式第5-9号)**. |
| 2 | 出入国する際の送迎 | Pickup from the airport on arrival, and escort to airport security on departure. | Pickup date (landing date), send-off date (departure date), and the staff member. |
| 3 | 住居確保・生活に必要な契約支援 | Acting as guarantor or providing company housing; help opening a bank account and signing up for a phone and utilities. | Housing details (address, type such as rented or company housing, rent), and a summary of the help with other contracts. |
| 4 | 生活オリエンテーション | Rules and manners in Japan, how to use public services, what to do in a disaster, and similar topics. | Date/time and place, content, method, and staff (including the interpreter and whoever gave the legal-protection briefing). Keep the **生活オリエンテーションの確認書 (参考様式第5-8号)**. |
| 5 | 公的手続等への同行 | Going with the worker to residence, social insurance and tax procedures as needed, and help with the paperwork. | Recorded under item 4: "including accompanying the worker to public offices" (関係機関への同行に関する事項を含む). |
| 6 | 日本語学習の機会の提供 | Information about Japanese classes and learning materials. | Content, method, and staff (including any contracted teacher). |
| 7 | 相談・苦情への対応 | Handling consultations and complaints in a language the worker fully understands, with advice and guidance. | Date/time, what was raised and how it was handled, any reports or referrals to authorities (date and office), and staff. Keep the **相談記録書 (参考様式第5-4号)**. |
| 8 | 日本人との交流促進 | Information about community events, neighbourhood associations and local festivals, and help taking part. | Date/time and place, method (what was promoted), and the staff member's name and title. |
| 9 | 転職支援（人員整理等の場合） | When the employer ends the contract: help finding a new job, a reference letter, paid leave for job hunting, and information on the procedures. | Date/time and place of each job-change consultation, content and handling, Hello Work visits (date and office), candidate employers, and staff. |
| 10 | 定期的な面談・行政機関への通報 | Meetings with the worker and with their supervisor **at least once every 3 months**, plus reporting any labour-law or immigration violations. | Supervisor's name and title, meeting date/time, content and handling, and staff. Keep the **定期面談報告書 (参考様式第5-5号 for the worker, 第5-6号 for the supervisor)**. |

The same section also requires four management ledgers (管理簿): (1) the support set-up, meaning the office, staff, monthly number of workers supported and missing workers, the support manager and staff with their CVs, and the languages offered; (2) the support outsourcing contract, including the 支援委託契約書 (参考様式第5-10号) and support costs; (3) the workers supported, including the plan and start/end dates; and (4) the support delivered, which is the item list above.
https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001468647.pdf (pp.86–88)

Online meetings for item 10 need the worker's consent, and the consent document must be kept as a ledger record. A recording of each online meeting must be kept **for at least 1 year after the employment contract ends** and shown to the regional immigration bureau on request.
Source: 運用要領別冊「１号特定技能外国人支援に関する運用要領」 (last amended 令和7年4月1日 / 2025-04-01). https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/930004553.pdf

### 1.2 How long records must be kept

- **Employer:** it must write up the support records and keep them at the office where support is given **for at least 1 year from the end of the 特定技能雇用契約**. The rule comes from 特定技能基準省令 第2条第2項第3号. Electronic records are allowed if they can be printed straight away.
  Source: 運用要領, 令和8年10月 edition, p.86. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001468647.pdf
- **登録支援機関:** the same rule applies to it. It must keep the records for **1 year from the end of the worker's employment contract** and must be able to produce them when asked for a report or documents. Its record list also includes the 定期面談報告書 (第5-5号 and 第5-6号).
  Source: 運用要領, 令和8年10月 edition, pp.150–152. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001468647.pdf

### 1.3 The periodic report (定期届出): how often, which form, which fields

**How often: confirmed, it changed from quarterly to once a year.**
- ISA's filing page, in its notice dated 2025-04-16, says the quarterly report due by 2025-04-15 was the last one. The next report would come in April 2026 or later, and from then on the report is filed once a year. The same page records the 2025-04-01 amendment of the 運用要領 that changed how reports work.
  https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/ssw/nyuukokukanri10_00002.html (retrieved 2026-11-22)
- Under the current rules, the report covers 1 April to 31 March and is due **between 1 April and 31 May of the following year**. If 31 May falls on a weekend or holiday, the deadline moves to the next working day. The first annual report covered 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31 and was filed between 2026-04-01 and 2026-05-31. **The next one, covering 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31, is due 2027-04-01 to 2027-05-31.**
  Sources: the same filing page; 定期届出作成要領 (published 2026-01-26), pp.1 and 4. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001454514.pdf
- The legal basis is 施行規則 第19条の18第3項: the report is due "毎年５月３１日までに", covering the previous 1 April to 31 March.
  Source: 運用要領, 令和8年10月 edition, 第7節, pp.122–124. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001468647.pdf
- **Who files:** the employer. When support is fully outsourced, the 登録支援機関 reports *through* the employer, and the employer gathers the 登録支援機関's support status and submits it with its own report. If an employer does not file, it cannot take on 特定技能 workers. If a 登録支援機関's support status is not submitted, its registration can be cancelled. Filing is through the online notification system (電子届出システム), which ISA recommends, or on paper to the regional bureau.
  Sources: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/ssw/nyuukokukanri10_00002.html and https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001454514.pdf
- **More rule changes from 2026-04-01:** employers no longer attach wage ledgers. Employers that do not meet a set of criteria (一定の基準) must now attach labour insurance, social insurance and tax payment records.
  Source: ISA leaflet 「特定技能制度の定期届出のルール変更」 (no date on file; the filing page dates it 2026-03-19). https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001441278.pdf

**Forms.** The new versions apply to reports filed from 2026-04-01 (令和8年4月1日以降に提出する際に使用する新様式):
- 参考様式第3-6号 「受入れ・活動・支援実施状況に係る届出書」 (main form): https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001454511.xlsx
- 参考様式第3-6号（別紙1） 「特定技能外国人の受入れ・活動・支援実施状況」 (one sheet per workplace): https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001454512.xlsx
- 参考様式第3-6号（別紙2） signature sheet, needed when several 登録支援機関 are used: https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001454513.xlsx
- These are listed on ISA's forms page (retrieved 2026-11-22): https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/ssw/10_00020.html

**Fields.**
- Main form 第3-6号 (作成要領 pp.4–13, and 運用要領 p.124):
  - the employer's corporate number, industry field(s), name and address
  - the number of 1号 and 2号 workers during the period
  - working conditions as monthly averages per worker: days worked, scheduled hours, overtime hours, regular cash pay (with overtime, commuting, attendance and family allowances shown separately), bonuses, deductions (food, housing including utilities, tax and social insurance, other), and pay-rise rate
  - support status: either "自社支援のみ" (in-house only) or "支援の全部委託あり" (at least one worker fully outsourced)
  - remarks
  - contact person, and the signatures of the employer and the 登録支援機関
- 別紙1, one row per worker (read from the Excel form):
  - name, nationality/region, date of birth, sex and residence card number (12 digits)
  - field
  - **total days worked in the year**
  - **total pay for the year**
  - pay method (bank transfer, or "other", which needs a 報酬支払証明書, 参考様式第5-7号)
  - **support form** (in-house, or fully outsourced plus the 登録支援機関's name)
  - **support staff member's name and phone number** as of 31 March
  - a **checkbox for 随時届出（報告）の提出状況**. It is ticked when something made a mandatory support item hard to deliver but the required ad-hoc report (参考様式第3-7号 for an employer, 第4-3号 特異事案報告 for a 登録支援機関) was not filed. In that case a written explanation must be attached.
  - remarks, including start and end dates
  - The back of the sheet only restates the 10 mandatory items.

**Important for the product.** The new annual form **does not ask for meeting dates, consultation content or community-contact activities**. Those details live in the records kept on site (§1.2): the 定期面談報告書, 相談記録書 and so on. The bureau can ask to see them. The annual report only asks how support was delivered (in-house or outsourced), who the contact person is, and whether any required ad-hoc report was missed.

## 3. Registered support organisations (登録支援機関)

- **Nationwide: 11,876 registered as of 2026-11-19.** The register leaves out organisations whose registration was cancelled.
  Source: ISA 「登録支援機関登録簿」. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/applications/ssw/nyuukokukanri07_00205.html
- **Nagasaki: 79** organisations have their registered address (住所) in 長崎県. **76** list a support office (支援を行う事務所) in 長崎県. These counts are mine, taken from the register Excel dated 2026-11-19. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001474664.xlsx
  - ISA publishes no prefecture total, so this is my count of the "住所" and "支援を行う事務所" columns. Organisations based in other prefectures can also support workers in Nagasaki.
- Registration lasts 5 years. The fee is ¥28,400 for a new registration and ¥11,100 to renew.
  https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/policies/ssw/supportssw.html

## 4. What employers pay a 登録支援機関 per worker per month

**Official figure (ISA):**
- The **average monthly support fee per 特定技能 worker is ¥28,386**, and about 90% of fees are ¥30,000 or less.
- The data counts 1号 workers in Japan at the end of September 2022 (令和4年9月末, preliminary figures) whose latest 1号 application said support would be outsourced to a 登録支援機関 for a fee. That is 70,404 workers.
- Distribution:

| Monthly fee per worker | Share |
|---|---|
| ≤¥5,000 | 0.9% |
| ¥5,001–10,000 | 6.4% |
| ¥10,001–15,000 | 9.5% |
| ¥15,001–20,000 | 25.3% |
| ¥20,001–25,000 | 26.2% (largest band) |
| ¥25,001–30,000 | 20.3% |
| >¥30,000 | 11.5% |

- Source: ISA, 「特定技能制度の現状について」, 参考資料4, 技能実習制度及び特定技能制度の在り方に関する有識者会議（第8回）, 2023-06-14, p.33. https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001397709.pdf
- The meeting date is confirmed by the agenda (議事次第): https://www.moj.go.jp/isa/content/001397514.pdf
- The source does not say whether the fees include tax.

**Vendor claims (not official):** the vendor pages found repeat the ISA figure and add that set-up fees are usually charged on top.
- MEIKOGLOBAL (updated 2026-10-06) says the average is ¥28,386, that 71.8% of fees fall between ¥15,000 and ¥30,000, and that the pre-arrival guidance and life orientation are usually billed separately as set-up costs. https://meikoglobal.jp/magazine/average-costs-for-registered-support-organizations/
- TreeGlobalPartners (no date shown, retrieved 2026-11-22) gives "about ¥20,000–30,000 per person per month", says "most cases" add set-up costs, and quotes "about ¥20,000–50,000 per person" elsewhere on the page. https://treeglobalpartners.com/articles/torokushien-kikan-hiyo/
- Tomohata (retrieved 2026-11-22) says that, as of 2026-09-12, it could find no national figure newer than September 2022. https://tomohata.jp/guide/shien-kikan-hiyou

## 5. Google Gemini API paid-tier pricing (USD per 1M tokens)

Source: Gemini API pricing page (retrieved 2026-11-22; the page shows no "last updated" date). https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing now redirects to https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing. Both model IDs the team uses appear on the page under exactly these names.

| Model (as listed) | Tier | Input (text/image/video) | Input (audio) | Output (incl. thinking) | Context cache |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini 3.8 Flash** `gemini-3.8-flash` | Standard | **$0.75 through 2026-12-31; $1.50 from 2027-01-01** | No separate audio rate is listed; the page shows one input price | **$3.75 through 2026-12-31; $7.50 from 2027-01-01** | $0.075, then $0.15; storage $0.50/1M tokens/hour, then $1.00 |
| | Batch / Flex | $0.375, then $0.75 | (same single input price) | $1.875, then $3.75 | $0.0375, then $0.075 |
| | Priority | $1.35, then $2.70 | (same single input price) | $6.75, then $13.50 | $0.135, then $0.27 |
| **Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite** `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` | Standard | **$0.30** (text / image / video / audio) | **$0.30** (same rate) | **$2.50** | $0.03; storage $1.00/1M tokens/hour |
| | Batch | $0.15 | $0.15 | $1.25 | $0.015 |

- **The 3.8 Flash price doubles on 2027-01-01.** Cost estimates for anything after the hackathon should use $1.50 input and $7.50 output.
- The free tier is "Free of charge" for both models, and free-tier data is "Used to improve our products: Yes". On the paid tier it is "No". https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing
- For context, the page also lists `gemini-3.6-flash`, described as the "previous generation Flash", and `gemini-3.1-flash-lite`. Requests to the deprecated `gemini-2.5-flash` now route to 3.5 Flash-Lite and are billed at its rates. https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing

**Audio token rate:**
- Audio understanding (audio sent as input to the model) uses **32 tokens per second of audio, so 1 minute is 1,920 tokens**. The maximum is 9.5 hours of audio per prompt.
  Source: Gemini API audio docs, "Technical details about audio" (retrieved 2026-11-22). https://aistudio.google.com/docs/audio
  The token docs say the same ("Audio: 32 tokens per second"): https://aistudio.google.com/docs/tokens
- Live and real-time transcription models are billed at **25 tokens per second** of audio, per the pricing page footnotes for the Live and Transcribe models. This rate does not apply to the standard Flash and Flash-Lite calls. https://aistudio.google.com/docs/pricing
- Worked example, my arithmetic: 1 minute of audio on 3.5 Flash-Lite is 1,920 tokens × $0.30 / 1M ≈ $0.00058 of input.
