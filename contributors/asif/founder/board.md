Run of `/founder-board` (founder-skill v1.0.0) on 2026-11-22. Memos: [offers](board/offers.md) · [monopoly](board/monopoly.md) · [product](board/product.md) · brief: [board/brief.md](board/brief.md). The lenses apply published frameworks; the members are not the authors of those frameworks.

## 1. The vote

**3 × FUND IF, 0 pass. Average score 4.3 / 10** (Offers 4, Monopoly 4, Product 5).

## 2. Risks raised by more than one member (most dangerous first)

1. **Nobody has shown the payer will pay.** (Offers, Monopoly) The 登録支援機関 has no price, no named organisation has been asked, and nobody has confirmed that the Support Log matches what it must file or that the Immigration Services Agency would accept it. The factory uses the app; someone else pays; nothing connects the two yet.
2. **The software lock may not survive a real factory.** (all three) Unknown whether a plant will stand a tablet in front of a machine; a worker can walk past LOCK; a wrong translation that a worker "passes" is worse than no gate. If the lock goes, the centre of the product goes.
3. **The foreman carries the effort and gets nothing for it.** (Offers, Product) Recording, checking drafts and re-approving every machine sits on the busiest person in the chain, at a factory that pays nothing and can drop it in a busy week. Every approval voids that machine's Sign-offs, adding friction where adoption is decided.
4. **The AI is a commodity and the free tier cannot run one factory.** (Monopoly, Product, Offers) Translation and quiz generation run on a model every competitor can use; about 20 calls a day will not survive a real shift; shop jargon (玉掛け, 段取り) is where drafts go wrong, and nobody has measured the error rate.
5. **It is copyable.** (Monopoly) tebiki, Teachme Biz and カミナシ already translate and quiz; a "gate by machine" screen is a feature an incumbent could ship in months.

Raised by one member only: **two products in one** (Product): machine-gated shop training and neighbourhood life support pull in different directions; the pitch needs a paragraph.

## 3. Conditions, merged (the checklist for the rest of the pack)

- [ ] **Payer proof:** at least one (Offers asks three) 登録支援機関 names a price or signs a letter of intent, and states what it spends today on orientation, consultation and exchange support. → `/founder-pricing`, `/founder-consumer`
- [ ] **Compliance fit:** the Support Log checked field by field against the 特定技能 support filing requirements (運用要領) by someone who files them. → desk check now, a support organisation later
- [ ] **One real machine:** one factory runs the lock panel on one real machine with one real foreman for a set trial; record time per instruction and edits per draft. → `/founder-launch`
- [ ] **One product, one sentence:** pick the half that the payer pays for and the factory cannot live without; set the other aside or make it the bonus. → `/founder-offer`
- [ ] **Beachhead size:** number of target factories and 登録支援機関 in Nagasaki, and the year-one share. → `/founder-competitors`, open question
- [ ] **Moat plan:** turn the cross-factory record of misunderstood terms into something an incumbent cannot copy within a year. → `/founder-competitors`
- [ ] **AI cost model:** cost per factory per month beyond the free tier; measured glossary error rate on 玉掛け / 段取り. → `/founder-cfo`, WS1 (Galib)
- [ ] **Bypass answer:** what happens when a worker ignores LOCK, who notices, and how. → product decision
- [ ] **Guarantee:** e.g. "if the log does not cover your required support records, you pay nothing." → `/founder-offer`

## 4. The strongest version the board can see

The members disagree on which half leads, and that disagreement is the finding. The Product lens says the best moment is on the factory floor (scan → LOCK → pass → READY, with the exact misunderstood term sent to the foreman) and wants everything else cut until that loop is loved. The Offers lens says the money is in the payer's legal duty, which sits mostly in the Life half, and wants the pitch to lead with a Support Log stack sold to the support organisation, with the machine lock as the bonus that keeps factories using it. Put together, the strongest business is **a compliance-and-safety record that a 登録支援機関 buys per worker, whose daily data comes from the machine gate**: the gate is why foremen and workers open the app every day, and the Support Log is why someone pays. That is narrower than the pitch: the Skill Matrix, Heatmap and Knowledge Library are support features, not the product. Every member's score rises only with evidence the team does not have yet: a named payer at a named price, and one real machine in one real factory.

## What this means for the hackathon (chair's note, not a board vote)

The board judges a business, and its conditions (letters of intent, a factory pilot) cannot be met by Nov 24. What the team can do before the deadline, in the proposal and the demo:
1. Say the one sentence the board asked for, and lead with the machine-gate moment.
2. Name the payer and the duty honestly ("support organisations must provide…; the Support Log is the evidence") and mark the price and the pilot as next steps, not facts.
3. Answer the bypass question on stage: the Foreman board flags an assigned Worker without a valid Sign-off (already in the plan).
4. Show the AI's measured result on the seeded Glossary Terms (Galib's WS1 tests) instead of claiming accuracy.
