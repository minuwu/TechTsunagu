# Wakatta? (わかった？)

- What it is: a web app that turns a foreman's 20-second Japanese voice note into a plain-Japanese, Bengali and English instruction pinned to one machine, and keeps that machine's tablet panel LOCKED for a foreign worker until they pass a short quiz on it; wrong answers alert the foreman with the exact shop word (玉掛け, 段取り…) that was misunderstood. The same app turns neighbourhood notices (garbage rules, drills, festivals) into plain steps with calendar dates, answers workers' questions only from those notices, and pairs each worker with a Japanese "Senpai".
- Who it is for: small and mid-sized manufacturers in Nagasaki / Kyushu that employ 特定技能 or 技能実習 workers (often from Bangladesh, Vietnam, Indonesia). The factory owner or plant manager decides to adopt it; foremen (班長) and workers use it daily.
- What it sells, at what price: free for employers and workers. Paid by 登録支援機関 (registered support organisations), which by law must give 特定技能1号 workers life orientation, consultation and exchange support; the app's Support Log is evidence of that support. The support organisation's price is **not set yet** (open question for pricing).
- Where and how: Japan, first Nagasaki Prefecture. Delivered as a phone/tablet web app (QR sticker on each machine, no install). Found through support organisations and the prefecture's foreign-talent programmes.
- Budget and constraints: hackathon project (BJIT TSUNAGU hackathon, build window ends 2026-11-24; judges: Socio Future Inc., Daishin Giken Co., Nagasaki Prefecture). Team of 5 students, Azure student credits, Gemini free tier. No customers, no pilots and no revenue yet.

## Evidence the team already has (from the plan, sourced)

- MHLW 2025 外国人雇用実態調査: 46.2% of employers name 「日本語能力等のためにコミュニケーションが取りにくい」 as a challenge, the most-cited one (up from 43.9%) — https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_75722.html
- Injury rates per 1,000 workers: 3.98 (技能実習), 3.91 (特定技能), 2.3 (all workers) (the plan lists the final table as still to verify).
- Existing tools already translate manuals and run quizzes: tebiki, Teachme Biz, カミナシ. The team's claimed difference: the quiz gates a specific machine through its panel, and every wrong answer reaches the foreman with the exact term.
