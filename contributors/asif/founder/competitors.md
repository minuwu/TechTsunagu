## 2. Price range

Most direct competitors do not publish prices: カミナシ 教育, tebiki, Skillnote and VideoStep are all "quote on request". The figures below therefore rest on only a few data points.

- **Factory training and manual SaaS, monthly (3 public prices):** lowest ¥34,000 ([MANAVO](https://ascii.jp/elem/000/004/424/4424314/)), median ¥35,000 ([ヘルプドッグ Light](https://www.itreview.jp/products/tebiki/price)), highest ¥89,800 ([Teachme Biz Entry](https://biz.teachme.jp/price/)). The older Teachme plans ran from ¥59,800 to ¥319,800 and are closed to new customers. Teachme also sold a ¥30,000/month auto-translation add-on on those old plans.
- **Support-organisation software (1 public price):** [Linkus](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/itselect/employment-management-system-for-foreigners/) charges ¥16,500/month plus ¥1,078 per worker per month, tax included. For 10 workers that comes to ¥27,280/month (arithmetic from the published rates).
- **Substitutes:** VoiceTra and Google翻訳 are free. Pocketalk is ¥36,300 per device, one-off. An interpreter costs ¥25,000 to ¥35,000 or more per day.

This matters for the payer: a 登録支援機関 already pays per worker for support-record software in the ¥1,000/worker/month range, and a factory already pays ¥34,000 to ¥90,000/month for training SaaS. Wakatta? is free to the factory, so it undercuts every direct price. The support-organisation price still needs to sit near the Linkus per-worker rate.

## 5. The team's claim, tested

The claim: "tebiki, Teachme Biz, カミナシ already translate manuals and run quizzes; nobody gates a specific machine through a panel and alerts the foreman with the exact misunderstood term."

**Part 1 (they translate and run quizzes): mostly true, but wrong for tebiki.**
- カミナシ 教育 has multiple-choice tests, [translated into 18 languages since 2025-12-09](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/articles/20251209_My%E3%82%AB%E3%83%9F%E3%83%8A%E3%82%B7%E3%81%AE%E3%83%86%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88%E3%81%8C%E5%A4%9A%E8%A8%80%E8%AA%9E%E7%BF%BB%E8%A8%B3%E3%81%AB%E5%AF%BE%E5%BF%9C%E3%81%97%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F-61e7f53c).
- Teachme Biz added [comprehension-test auto-generation (β)](https://it.impress.co.jp/articles/-/27635).
- For tebiki I found translation and a skill map but **no quiz feature**, on either [its site](https://tebiki.jp/) or the listings I searched. That means not found, not proven absent.

**Part 2 (nobody gates a machine): held for Japan. Pieces exist nearby.**
- QR codes at the point of work already exist: a Teachme Biz user posts QR codes on site so staff open the manual where they work. They wrote 「QRコード出力をし、業務シーンに合った場所に貼っておくことで、すぐにマニュアルを閲覧できるのが良い。」 ([ITreview](https://www.itreview.jp/products/teachme-biz/reviews/174291)). That reviewer works in hospitality, not a factory.
- カミナシ leaves gating to the manager. Its launch note says 「テスト結果をふまえて従業員の理解度に応じた業務を担当してもらうことも可能です」 ([press release](https://corp.kaminashi.jp/news/pr_20251009)). That is a manual decision, not a lock.
- カミナシ already runs shared tablets and kiosk terminals on the floor: [shared-device translation, 2026-07-09](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/articles/20260709_%E5%85%B1%E7%94%A8%E7%AB%AF%E6%9C%AB%E3%81%A7%E5%A4%9A%E8%A8%80%E8%AA%9E%E7%BF%BB%E8%A8%B3%E3%81%8C%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B%E3%82%88%E3%81%86%E3%81%AB%E3%81%AA%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F-28d71046), [bulk kiosk registration](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/articles/20260819_%E3%82%AD%E3%82%AA%E3%82%B9%E3%82%AF%E7%AB%AF%E6%9C%AB%E3%82%92%E4%B8%80%E6%8B%AC%E7%99%BB%E9%8C%B2%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B%E3%82%88%E3%81%86%E3%81%AB%E3%81%AA%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F-04dba833).
- Outside Japan, gating equipment by training record already exists in hardware. An RFID vendor (ELATEC) wrote: "An operator whose certification is incomplete or has lapsed is not able to start the equipment." ([MMH, 2019](https://www.mmh.com/article/other_voices_leveraging_rfid_user_authentication_and_access_control_for_mat/Safety)). That is forklift access control, not a quiz.
- [Augmentir](https://www.augmentir.ai/product/training) schedules work "based on proficiency and skill-levels". I found no blocking feature.

**Part 3 (nobody alerts the foreman with the exact misunderstood term): held.** I found no product that extracts the misunderstood shop term and pushes it to the foreman.
- The closest is カミナシ. Its [2025-10-14 note](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/articles/20251014_%E3%83%86%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88%E3%81%AE%E5%9B%9E%E7%AD%94%E7%B5%90%E6%9E%9C%E3%82%92CSV%E3%83%95%E3%82%A1%E3%82%A4%E3%83%AB%E3%81%A7%E3%83%80%E3%82%A6%E3%83%B3%E3%83%AD%E3%83%BC%E3%83%89%E3%81%A7%E3%81%8D%E3%82%8B%E3%82%88%E3%81%86%E3%81%AB%E3%81%AA%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F-0010811f) lets managers download a CSV of each learner's selected answers, which is per-question data pulled by the manager.
- Its [2025-10-06 note](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/articles/20251006_%E4%B8%8D%E6%AD%A3%E8%A7%A3%E3%81%8C%E3%81%82%E3%81%A3%E3%81%9F%E5%A0%B4%E5%90%88%E5%8F%97%E8%AC%9B%E8%80%85%E3%81%B8%E5%86%8D%E3%83%86%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88%E3%82%92%E4%BF%83%E3%81%9B%E3%82%8B%E3%82%88%E3%81%86%E3%81%AB%E3%81%AA%E3%82%8A%E3%81%BE%E3%81%97%E3%81%9F-d88163a5) sends wrong answers back to the learner for a retest, not to the manager.
- Its 2026 release notes show no manager-notification release ([2026 list](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/categories/2026%E5%B9%B4%E3%83%AA%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9-9318fa48)).

**Two things in the idea are not unique:**
- **Bengali:** カミナシ 従業員 [added Bengali in 2025-04](https://kaminashi.jp/news/detail/pr_20250417_1).
- **Machine-side QR:** Teachme Biz users already post QR codes on site, as quoted above.

## 6. THE GAP

The gap is a narrow one, so state it narrowly.

No product found ties a comprehension check to one specific machine and holds that machine's instructions locked until the worker passes. None tells the 班長 which shop word (玉掛け, 段取り…) the worker got wrong. None writes that event into the support record the 登録支援機関 must keep. The evidence:
- Translation output that workers cannot read is the top complaint in the most direct competitor: 5 of 8 カミナシ 教育 reviews.
- カミナシ stops at "the manager may assign work by result" and a CSV download.
- Support-record software (Linkus, かんべえ, トクビザ) holds 面談 records, but nothing in it shows workplace comprehension.
- Free public materials skip Bengali ([MHLW: 13 languages, no Bengali](https://anzeninfo.mhlw.go.jp/information/kyozaishiryo.html)), and no public price exists for any direct tool at the small-factory end except ¥34k–¥90k/month.

The gap is a **feature combination plus a payer model** (free for the factory, paid by the support organisation). It is not a new category. No public review asks for it, so demand is unproven, and the panel lock is a soft lock on a tablet, not a machine interlock.

## 7. THE THREAT

**カミナシ is the fastest copier.** It already has every part:
- tests translated into 18 languages;
- per-answer data;
- shared tablets and kiosks on the floor;
- an equipment-record product ([カミナシ 設備保全, 設備カルテ](https://kaminashi.jp/));
- a worker app that supports Bengali.

Joining "test passed" to "equipment record", and adding a push alert on the wrong option, is a feature release, not a new product. It ships test features almost monthly (2026-01-06, 02-12, 10-22 and 11-12 in its [2026 list](https://docs.channel.io/training/ja/categories/2026%E5%B9%B4%E3%83%AA%E3%83%AA%E3%83%BC%E3%82%B9-9318fa48)). One 2026-11-19 note is titled 「VideoStepの翻訳対応言語を追加しました」. That suggests VideoStep now sits in カミナシ's training line, though I did not verify it.

**Second threat: on the payer side.** [Linkus](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/itselect/employment-management-system-for-foreigners/) already gives the employer and the 登録支援機関 separate accounts on one system and charges per worker. It could add a worker-facing notice translator and a "comprehension log" to its support records. That would take the payer before Wakatta? reaches it.

**What would slow a copier:** owning the foreman's 20-second voice note to instruction loop, and per-term misunderstanding data across many factories in one region (Nagasaki). Only the data is hard to copy, and it does not exist yet.
