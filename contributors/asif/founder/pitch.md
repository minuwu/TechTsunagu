Wakatta? is a phone and tablet web app for factories with foreign workers. No install: a QR sticker goes on each machine.
A foreman records a 20-second voice note at a machine; each worker gets it in やさしい日本語, Bengali or English, with a Japanese back-translation the foreman can check, and answers 2 or 3 questions on it.
The tablet beside the machine shows LOCK for a worker until they pass; any wrong answer tells the foreman which shop word (玉掛け, 段取り…) was misunderstood.
Workers also get neighbourhood notices (garbage rules, drills) as plain steps with dates, and you can export a support log for your 特定技能 support duties.
Free for your company and your workers; it is paid for by registered support organisations (登録支援機関). Answer price questions in yen per worker per month.
